Research

Since March 2026 I've been an undergraduate researcher in Dr. Kristen Vaccaro's lab at UC San Diego, on a project funded by the National Science Foundation.

LLM-as-judge pipeline

I built a pipeline that uses a large language model as a judge: the model scores or labels other text so it can be evaluated at scale instead of entirely by hand.

Stance classifier

I built a transformer-based classifier for stance detection: deciding whether a piece of text supports, opposes or is neutral toward a topic.
